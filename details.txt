INVESTORS CLUB
FOUNDER PROFILE
Dr. MARTHA RAMAKRISHNA
Founder | Entrepreneur | Construction Professional | Community Development Visionary
 
About the Founder
Dr. Martha Ramakrishna is an entrepreneur and construction professional with more than 20 years of practical experience in the construction sector. His professional journey began at the age of 14, when he entered the construction field as a daily-wage worker after discontinuing his studies.
Driven by a simple ambition to build a home of his own, he worked and saved for six years and, at the age of 20, built his first house with his hard-earned income. This early experience shaped his understanding of hard work, construction, entrepreneurship and the value of creating opportunities from limited resources.
Over the years, Martha expanded his professional journey into construction, transport, social initiatives and public engagement. In 2014, he contested the Assembly election from the Kodada constituency. In 2015, he started Skillful Golden Telangana, an initiative focused on English coaching and skill development for young people.
In 2019, he established SS & N Constructions in Khammam, through which he has been involved in commercial, educational, healthcare, residential and hospitality construction projects, including malls, supermarkets, schools, colleges, hospitals, apartments and hotels.
Beyond Construction
His interests extend beyond construction. He has written about agriculture, farmers and rural development, and has developed the proposed “119 New Townships in Telangana” concept, envisioning integrated communities combining housing, education, healthcare, commercial facilities and infrastructure.
The Vision Behind the Investors Club
The Investors Club is his next step toward creating a platform where ideas, capital and management come together.
The concept is designed to bring together idea creators, investors and professionals or managers so that people with business ideas can connect with people who can provide financial resources and people who can contribute expertise, operations and execution.
IDEAS  +  INVESTMENT  +  MANAGEMENT  =  OPPORTUNITY FOR EXECUTION
The Investors Club is envisioned as a collaborative environment where entrepreneurs can present ideas, investors can explore opportunities, and capable professionals can participate in building and managing ventures.

His public and community-oriented interests include skill development, youth opportunities, agriculture, farmer welfare, rural development and integrated community development. His political interests are presented as part of his public-service engagement and personal vision for development.
